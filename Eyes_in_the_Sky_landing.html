Six phases, one pipeline

From raw patches to an explained model. The line fills in as you scroll.

  1. Understand the data

    Class balance, sample grids and colour histograms, then one fixed stratified 70/15/15 split.

    01_eda.ipynb

  2. Build a baseline CNN

    A network built from scratch for 64×64×3 patches, with at least two ablations such as depth, BatchNorm or dropout.

    02_baseline_cnn.ipynb

  3. Borrow pre-trained eyes

    Fine-tune two ImageNet models and compare a frozen backbone with full fine-tuning.

    03_transfer_learning.ipynb

  4. Measure data efficiency

    Train on 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100% of the data, repeating the small sizes with 2–3 seeds.

    src/train.py

  5. Evaluate and explain

    Confusion matrix, per-class F1, Grad-CAM for every class and a t-SNE of the embeddings.

    04_evaluation_and_explainability.ipynb

  6. Document and submit

    README, report and a public repository with commits from all five members.

    README.md

What can we do for you today?

A CNN reads 64×64 pixel Sentinel-2 patches and names one of ten land-use classes. Start the story and watch each square get labelled.

We compare a scratch CNN with fine-tuned pre-trained models (frozen vs full), then retrain on 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100% of the data, using the same test set and several seeds at small sizes. Jump to Borrowing Eyes.

Group 5: Tejash, Shrivalli, Rachana, Rohan and Nitin (team leader). Deliverables are a PDF report and a public GitHub repository, with every member committing from their own account. Meet the team.
